# Voss's Repeat Engagement

*2nd-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, contingent on a new Statement of Work being
executed<br>
**Range/Area:** Any target of a Reorg or Voss's Flattened Hierarchy whose
duration has already ended<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a new engagement letter, consumed; a consulting
fee equal to or greater than the original)<br>
**Duration:** Same as the spell it recasts<br>
**Classes:** COO, VP of People, Chief of Staff

You recast an expired Reorg or Voss's Flattened Hierarchy on the same
target at full effect, without diagnosing what caused the original casting
to lapse. No saving throw is required from any creature who already
succeeded against the original casting — success against this spell does
not carry over from the last one, on the theory that the underlying
condition has changed since then.

This spell can be cast on the same target an unlimited number of times, at
unlimited expense, provided each casting is preceded by a new engagement
letter.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot, and the consulting fee does not decrease
with familiarity.
