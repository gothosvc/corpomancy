# Move HQ to Austin

*9th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to announce, 6 to 18 months to actually vacate
the old location, during which the new headquarters exists mainly as a
press release<br>
**Range/Area:** Self (the organization's registered legal address and
primary office)<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a state incentive package, an amended corporate
tax filing, a building with a lobby large enough to photograph)<br>
**Duration:** Until the next relocation<br>
**Classes:** CEO, Board, Chief Financial Officer (as a ritual, contingent
on state incentive negotiations)

You instantaneously relocate your organization's legal and cultural
center of gravity to a new jurisdiction. This spell does not teleport any
creature currently employed by the organization; each affected creature
must independently decide whether to relocate.

A creature who elects not to relocate may attempt a Tenure saving throw to
remain employed remotely or out of a satellite office. On a failure, the
creature is treated as having voluntarily left the organization —
identical in effect to failing a Reduction in Force saving throw, but
without triggering any severance obligation, since the departure is not
classified as a dismissal.

For 1 year following the casting, the organization is also under the
effect of Rebrand (see `marketing-optics/rebrand.md`) at no additional
cost, regardless of whether it was separately cast.

---
**Flavor:** *"We're thrilled to plant roots in a city that shares our
values of innovation and growth."* — press release, dateline unchanged
from the last relocation
