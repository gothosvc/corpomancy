# Return to Office Mandate

*8th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to announce, 3 to 6 months of grace period
before enforcement begins, extended at least once<br>
**Range/Area:** Company-wide; every workstation the organization has ever
provisioned remotely<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a memo citing "collaboration" and "culture,"
consumed; an already-signed, under-occupied office lease, not
consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until rescinded by a future caster, which historically has
not happened<br>
**Classes:** CEO, Board, Chief People Officer (as a ritual, requiring
extensive legal review)

Each creature within the area of effect must make a Tenure saving throw.
On a success, the creature negotiates a documented exception and is
unaffected, though the exception is rarely extended to anyone else who
asks. On a failure, the creature is compelled to appear in person on a
fixed schedule regardless of any measurable change to their output.

A portion of the creatures who fail their save elect to leave the
organization voluntarily rather than comply. Resolve this identically to a
failed Reduction in Force saving throw, at no additional component cost to
the caster.

The organization does not gain the real-estate utilization the memo cited
as justification for this spell, regardless of the outcome.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a 9th-level spell slot, the spell
closes all previously granted exceptions retroactively and extends to
contractors.

---
**Flavor:** *"We believe in-person collaboration is core to our culture,
and we're excited to bring the team back together."* — memo, sent six
months after the office lease renewal was signed
