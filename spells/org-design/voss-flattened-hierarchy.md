# Voss's Flattened Hierarchy

*3rd-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though it is never billed as 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** One management layer within a division<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a slide titled "Simplifying How We Work,"
consumed; a consulting fee, paid regardless of outcome)<br>
**Duration:** 1 year, or until Voss is re-engaged (see Voss's Repeat
Engagement)<br>
**Classes:** COO, VP of People, Chief of Staff

This spell functions as Reorg, except its effect is narrowed to a single
named layer of middle management rather than an entire org chart. Each
manager in the chosen layer must succeed on a Tenure saving throw. On a
failure, the manager is dismissed and their direct reports are
redistributed one level up; on a success, the manager is redesignated an
"Individual Contributor" or moved laterally at unchanged compensation.

For the duration, any creature attempting to identify who approves a given
decision has disadvantage on the check, since approval authority was
redistributed but not actually clarified. When the duration ends, a layer
matching the eliminated one's function is reported to have re-formed,
usually under a different title, at a headcount within 10% of the
original.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may target one additional layer, in a division you can
perceive, for each slot level above 3rd.

**Flavor:** *"We're removing a layer of management to get closer to our
customers."* — internal memo, eleven months before an open req for
"Senior Manager, Customer Proximity"
