# Matrix Malfunction

*3rd-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** Instantaneous, though it is never actually cast on
purpose — it is a standing condition of any matrixed reporting
structure<br>
**Range/Area:** Self, one creature with more than one reporting line<br>
**Components:** V, S (two or more conflicting sets of direction, each
issued in good faith)<br>
**Duration:** Until the creature's reporting structure is un-matrixed,
which no Reorg on record has ever actually accomplished<br>
**Classes:** Functional Manager, Product Manager, Anyone With Two Bosses

Choose one creature with more than one direct manager. Each manager may
independently issue the creature a directive requiring its compliance; if
two or more directives conflict, the creature must choose which to honor,
and automatically fails to satisfy every manager who did not receive
priority.

No saving throw is available to the affected creature, since no single
creature holds the authority to resolve the conflict — resolving it would
require the two managers to coordinate directly, an action this spell's
environment does not support.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot; adding a third manager does not create
three times the clarity.
