# New Management Style

*2nd-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, immediately following a 45-minute read of a
business book or a Harvard Business Review article skimmed between
meetings<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a new vocabulary — "radical candor," "servant
leadership," "psychological safety" — used correctly perhaps 40% of the
time)<br>
**Duration:** Until the caster reads a different article<br>
**Classes:** Manager, Director, VP

You transmute your own management approach wholesale. Any standing
agreement, working norm, or feedback process established with a direct
report under a previous casting of this spell is immediately voided and
forgotten by the caster, though not by the direct report.

For the duration, affected direct reports have disadvantage on any check
to predict what kind of feedback, praise, or criticism they will receive
in a given situation, since the underlying judgment producing that
feedback has not actually changed — only the vocabulary layered on top of
it.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot.

---
**Flavor:** *"I just finished this incredible book on radical candor and
I really think it's going to change how this team operates."* — said, by
this same manager, roughly every eight months
