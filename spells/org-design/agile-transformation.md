# Agile Transformation

*6th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 8 hours (a Training), plus an ongoing ritual component
performed every morning (a Standup)<br>
**Range/Area:** Self (affects the caster's entire organization)<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a certification, a set of colored sticky notes,
and a burndown chart)<br>
**Duration:** Until the organization reverts under stress, typically
coinciding with the next major deadline<br>
**Classes:** VP of Engineering, Agile Coach

You impose a new set of rituals, titles, and terminology on your
organization's existing work, without altering the underlying scope,
staffing, or deadlines of that work. Roles are renamed, meetings are
renamed, and work units are renamed.

For the duration, all affected creatures report feeling more aligned and
efficient, but no measurable change occurs to the organization's actual
output velocity, which was already a made-up unit before this spell was
cast.

The spell's duration ends automatically, without the caster's input, the
first time the organization faces a deadline it cannot meet through
renaming alone. At that point, all affected creatures revert to their prior
processes exactly, but keep the new titles.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when cast
using a higher-level slot.
