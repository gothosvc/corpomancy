# Org Design & Process Change

*Classical tradition: Transmutation*

Field notes on Transmutation as practiced here: turning one thing into
another — reorgs, pivots, "digital transformation," contractor-to-FTE
conversions, process overhauls. The substance doesn't always change as
much as the label does, a distinction this discipline appears
constitutionally incapable of acknowledging.

**Field tone:** disruptive, faux-visionary, allergic to admitting nothing
changed. The caster transforms the shape of things, with mixed effects on
the substance.

**Vocabulary recorded in the field:** reorg, pivot, digital
transformation, org chart, dotted line, matrixed reporting, change
management, rightsizing, synergy, restructure, agile transformation, "new
operating model," return to office, in-person collaboration, business-
friendly state, radical candor, flavor-of-the-month management.
