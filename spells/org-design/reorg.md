# Reorg

*4th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to announce, 1 to 3 months to actually take
effect
**Range/Area:** Sight (the org chart)
**Components:** V, S, M (a new org chart, drawn before the old one is fully
understood)
**Duration:** Until the next Reorg
**Classes:** CEO, Chief of Staff, VP of People

You restructure the reporting lines, team boundaries, and titles of a
portion of your organization you can perceive. Affected creatures retain
their skills, knowledge, and relationships, but their formal reporting
structure, team membership, and often their physical seating changes
entirely.

For 4d6 days following this spell, all affected creatures operate at
disadvantage on checks requiring them to know who is responsible for a
given decision. This penalty does not apply to a creature who simply
continues doing what they were doing before, regardless of what the new
chart says.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or
higher, you may additionally rename the affected teams or functions without
changing anything else about them. This has no further mechanical effect.

---
**Flavor:** *"We're excited to announce some changes to better position us
for the future. Please see the attached updated org chart."* —
company-wide email
