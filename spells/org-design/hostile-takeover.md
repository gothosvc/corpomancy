# Hostile Takeover

*8th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to initiate (a Tender Offer), though the
acquisition takes 3 to 18 months to complete, contested at every stage
**Range/Area:** Special; one organization you can identify as a
competitor, or one whose Runway has already reached 0
**Components:** V, S, M (a tender offer, consumed; a war chest of capital
equal to the target's estimated valuation, consumed)
**Duration:** Permanent; the target's structure is transmuted into a
subsidiary of your own
**Classes:** CEO, board, Chief of Staff

Choose one organization meeting the range's requirements. Its leadership
must succeed on a Solvency saving throw, made using the collective Tenure
of its board and executive team. On a failed save, the target's ownership,
assets, and reporting structure are transmuted entirely into your
organization; its former leadership is treated as a Legacy Servant of your
organization from that point forward — still present, still occasionally
consulted, no longer in control of anything.

On a successful save, the target takes structural damage regardless, and
may attempt a countermeasure at its next opportunity to act.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 9th
level, you may target a privately held organization as well, provided you
can produce a war chest equal to its estimated valuation twice over (a
Hostile Bid, followed by a Sweetened Offer).
