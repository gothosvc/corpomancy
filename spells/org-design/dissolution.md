# Dissolution

*9th-level Org Design & Process Change*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to file (a Certificate of Dissolution), though
winding up the organization's affairs takes 6 months to several years<br>
**Range/Area:** Self (the entire organization)<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a plan of liquidation, consumed; unanimous board
consent)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; permanent<br>
**Classes:** Board, CEO, Liquidating Trustee

You transmute your organization out of existence. Every remaining Legacy
Servant is destroyed simultaneously, without requiring a System Sunset or
Total Rewrite and without dealing damage to any dependent system — nothing
remains to depend on it. Every employee's position ends at once; unlike
Reduction in Force, no successful saving throw redeploys a creature to a
reduced role or a different team, since no team remains to receive them.

Your remaining Runway, if any, is distributed to creditors and equity
holders in a fixed priority order that cannot be altered by this casting.
A creditor further down the order receives nothing if the distribution is
exhausted before reaching them, regardless of the size or validity of
their claim.

Any Golden Parachute, D&O Insurance, or Indemnification already in effect
at the time of casting continues to resolve exactly as though the
organization still existed, with the Liquidating Trustee standing in as
counterparty. A Nondisclosure remains binding on any creature it affected,
whether or not an organization still exists to enforce it.

This spell cannot be uncast. There is no equivalent spell to bring a
dissolved organization back into existence — only its name, trademarks,
and remaining intellectual property can be acquired by an unrelated party,
through an ordinary purchase rather than any spell in this collection.
