# Short Position

*4th-level Finance & Cost-Cutting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though the position must be held for 1d6
months before it can be closed<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one publicly traded organization you can
identify as a competitor<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (borrowed shares, consumed; a thesis document, not
consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the position is closed<br>
**Classes:** Fund Manager, Analyst

Choose a rival, publicly traded organization. You bet against its stock
price directly. At the end of the duration, the target must succeed on a
Solvency saving throw — checked against whether its Runway ran out, its
Blamelessness score cratered following a public Root Cause Analysis, or any
other Insolvent-adjacent event occurred during the duration. If it fails,
you gain a Windfall equal to the damage the target took. If it succeeds,
you instead take damage equal to the position's original cost, and the
target gains a Windfall of its own.

This spell rewards being correct about a rival's weakness and punishes
being wrong about it in equal, opposite measure; there is no partial
credit.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th
level or higher, you may publicly announce the position at the time of
casting (a Short Report), which itself functions as a Smear Campaign
against the target for the duration.

**Flavor:** *"We believe the market has significantly mispriced the risk
here."* — thesis document, page 1
