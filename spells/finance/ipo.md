# IPO

*9th-level Finance & Cost-Cutting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though the underlying process (roadshow,
filings) takes 6 to 12 months
**Range/Area:** Self (the entire organization)
**Components:** V, S, M (an S-1 filing, consumed; a ticker symbol, not
consumed)
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the effects are permanent
**Classes:** CEO, CFO, board (as a ritual requiring unanimous consent)

You convert your organization's entire ownership structure into liquid
capital in a single enormous casting. You gain a reserve of temporary
resources called a Windfall, equal to 100 times your caster level, which can
be spent freely to cast any other spell in this collection without
expending a spell slot, once, at any point in the next 180 days.

Once cast, your organization's Runway, Blamelessness score, and the
Documented status of all its records become visible to any creature who
wishes to inspect them, permanently, for as long as the organization
exists. You also lose a protection you may not have known you had: any
creature holding a sufficient stake in your organization may cast Activist
Campaign or Hostile Takeover against you without first needing to identify
you as a competitor.

This spell cannot be uncast. There is no equivalent spell to take an
organization private again below 9th level.
