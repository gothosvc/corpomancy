# 10b5-1 Plan

*4th-level Finance & Cost-Cutting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to file the plan, though it provides no cover
for any trade executed in the following 30 days (a Cooling-Off Period)<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a written trading schedule, filed before the
caster can be shown to have known the information the schedule will later
appear to have anticipated)<br>
**Duration:** Until the scheduled trades are fully executed<br>
**Classes:** CEO, C-Suite, Board-Appointed Executive

You execute a trade in your own organization's securities timed to
information not yet available to the public. Because the trade was
scheduled under this spell before the triggering information can be shown
to have reached you, any Discovery saving throw later attempted to
establish that the trade was made on the information, rather than the
schedule, automatically fails — regardless of how the filing date and the
information's actual origin compare. You gain a Windfall equal to 4d10
percent of the position's value at the time of casting.

This spell does not require the schedule to have been filed by anyone
other than you, or reviewed by anyone with the authority to question its
timing.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or
higher, you may amend the schedule once after filing, provided no third
party has yet inspected it.

trading plan adopted in accordance with Rule 10b5-1, at a time when the
executive was not in possession of material nonpublic information."* —
