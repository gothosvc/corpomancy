# Impossible Deadline

*4th-level Finance & Cost-Cutting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though the date itself was fixed on a slide
months before anyone affected heard about it<br>
**Range/Area:** One project or team you can perceive on a roadmap<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a launch date, and a calendar hold titled
"Alignment")<br>
**Duration:** Until the deadline passes or is quietly moved, whichever the
caster finds easier to admit to<br>
**Classes:** VP of Product, CEO, Board

Each creature assigned to the affected project must make a Tenure saving
throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 force damage at the end of
each week until the spell ends, as scope is held fixed against the
original timeline. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage, and
the deadline is quietly renegotiated for that creature alone, though never
announced as such.

This spell does not increase headcount, reduce scope, or extend the
timeline by itself. It only makes the gap between them somebody else's
problem.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or
higher, the effect extends to one additional dependent team for each slot
level above 4th, each rolling its own saving throw.
