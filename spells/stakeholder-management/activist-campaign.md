# Activist Campaign

*8th-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to file (a 13D), though the campaign runs 3 to
12 months
**Range/Area:** Special; one organization in which you hold a stake, or
can acquire one
**Components:** V, S, M (a white paper detailing the target's failures,
consumed; a stake in the target organization, not consumed)
**Duration:** Until a shareholder vote is held or the target capitulates,
whichever comes first
**Classes:** Activist Investor, Fund Manager

Choose one organization in which you hold a qualifying stake. You attempt
to Charm Stakeholder every other shareholder of significant size
simultaneously; each must make a Tenure saving throw, with disadvantage if
the target's Blamelessness score is low or its Runway is running out. On a
failed save, that shareholder publicly sides with you.

If a majority of shareholders by value side with you, you may compel the
target to take one action within its power — replace an executive, adopt a
Reorg of your choosing, return capital to shareholders — as though you had
cast the relevant spell on their behalf.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 9th
level, you may simultaneously target the target's board directly with a
Tenure saving throw to remove one seated member, in addition to the
shareholder vote.
