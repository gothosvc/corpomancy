# Drive-By Comment

*1st-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically two words or fewer<br>
**Range/Area:** Anywhere the caster holds commenting permissions — a doc,
a PR, a Slack thread<br>
**Components:** V only (a short phrase: "plz fix," "not quite," "can we
simplify?")<br>
**Duration:** Until the caster elaborates, which this spell does not
require them to ever do<br>
**Classes:** Director, VP, Skip-Level Manager, C-Suite

Choose one piece of work you can perceive. No saving throw: the reporting
relationship between caster and target makes this compulsion
unresistable. The target must expend hours interpreting the comment's
intent, revising the work against each interpretation in turn, and
resubmitting for approval. A clarifying question directed back at the
caster fails automatically unless the caster chooses to answer it, which
is not compelled by this spell.

The spell's component cost does not scale with its effect: a caster who
expends a single word imposes the same obligation as one who writes a full
paragraph.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, you may apply the spell to one additional piece of work for each
slot level above 1st, simultaneously.
