# Power Word: Buzzword

*2nd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** 60 feet, or the length of a meeting agenda<br>
**Components:** V (a sufficient density of jargon — "synergy,"
"bandwidth," "circle back," "boil the ocean," and "low-hanging fruit," in
the same sentence)<br>
**Duration:** Until each affected creature acts on its own
interpretation<br>
**Classes:** Consultant, VP, Product Manager

This spell requires no saving throw. Each creature that hears the casting
privately constructs its own interpretation of what was actually decided,
confident that interpretation matches everyone else's in the room. This
spell has no effect on a creature whose role does not require it to act on
the meeting's outcome.

At the end of the spell's duration, each affected creature acts
independently on its own interpretation, producing as many divergent
outcomes as there were creatures affected — every one of them becomes
Misaligned. Previous compulsion effects in this collection at least agree
with themselves about what they compelled; this one doesn't.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, you may target a written document (a strategy memo, an OKR doc)
rather than a spoken meeting. The duration extends until the document is
next referenced by two different creatures simultaneously, at which point
the divergent interpretations surface publicly rather than quietly.

**Flavor:** *"Just to align — are we saying we're doing this, or...
exploring doing this?" "Great question. Let's take that offline."* —
meeting transcript, question never actually answered
