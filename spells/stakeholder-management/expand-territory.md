# Expand Territory

*3rd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically cast during a territory planning
cycle<br>
**Range/Area:** Self; any account currently assigned to another
salesperson within the same organization<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a CRM record, reassigned)<br>
**Duration:** Until the next territory planning cycle<br>
**Classes:** Account Executive, Sales Director, VP of Sales

Choose one account currently owned by another salesperson in your
organization. Make an attack roll against that salesperson's Tenure — your
seniority, or your manager's backing, serves as your attack bonus. On a
hit, the account and any associated commission are reassigned to you,
regardless of the relationship or pipeline work the original owner had
already built with it.

The original owner does not automatically learn the reassignment occurred
until the next commission statement, at which point they may formally
dispute it — a dispute resolved by the same manager who approved your
territory expansion in the first place.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may claim multiple accounts in a single casting, provided
they fall within a single vertical or region newly assigned to you.

---
**Flavor:** *"Just a heads up, given the new territory alignment, this
account's moving under me going forward — happy to loop you in on the
relationship!"* — Slack message, sent the same day the commission
statement posted
