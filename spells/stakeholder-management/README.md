# Sales & Stakeholder Management

*Classical tradition: Enchantment*

Field notes on Enchantment as practiced here: persuasion, buy-in, and
negotiation as literal mind-affecting magic. Sales, account management,
internal politics — anywhere the goal is to change what someone wants
rather than what's true. This is the discipline the Working Group argues
about most; several members maintain they are personally immune to it,
which we have started recording as a finding in its own right.

**Field tone:** charming, faintly manipulative, always closing. The caster
wins by changing minds, not by changing facts.

**Vocabulary recorded in the field:** buy-in, alignment, stakeholder map,
champion, close the deal, objection handling, rapport, executive sponsor,
win-win, consensus building, relationship capital, upsell, cross-sell,
"let's take this offline."
