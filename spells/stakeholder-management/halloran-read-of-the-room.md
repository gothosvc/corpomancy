# Halloran's Read of the Room

*2nd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, cast the moment a meeting ends<br>
**Range/Area:** One meeting or all-hands the caster attended<br>
**Components:** V, S (a confident nod; a summary email claiming
consensus)<br>
**Duration:** Until contradicted in writing<br>
**Classes:** VP of Strategy, Product Manager, anyone who ran the meeting

You declare the room's reception of a proposal, decision, or announcement
to be positive and aligned, regardless of the reactions actually present
during the meeting. Every creature who attended without objecting aloud is
treated as having agreed, and becomes Misaligned: privately certain of
whatever they actually understood the outcome to be, while the caster's
summary stands as the official record.

A creature who wishes to contest this spell's effect must do so in writing
before the summary is sent; an objection raised verbally during the
meeting, no matter how clearly stated, does not count.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, the summary may additionally be forwarded to a creature who was
not present, who is then treated as having agreed as well.

---
**Flavor:** *"Great alignment on this today — sending a recap shortly, but
wanted to confirm we're all moving forward as discussed."* — email, sent
to eleven people, four of whom spoke during the call
