# All-Hands

*5th-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour<br>
**Range/Area:** 120 feet, or the reach of a livestream<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a slide deck containing at least one aspirational
graphic of an upward-trending line with no axis labels)<br>
**Duration:** 30 days, or until the next All-Hands is cast, whichever is
shorter<br>
**Classes:** CEO, VP of Communications, Head of People

You address every creature able to see or hear you as a group. Each must
succeed on a Tenure saving throw or believe, for the duration, that the
organization's strategy is sound, that recent difficult decisions were
necessary, and that the future is bright, regardless of any information
available to that creature suggesting otherwise.

The effect ends early for an individual creature who receives direct,
contradicting evidence during the duration — for example, a Reduction in
Force affecting their own team.

This spell cannot be cast more than once per month without a cumulative
penalty: each additional casting within the same month imposes
disadvantage on its own saving throw's effectiveness, as affected creatures
begin discounting future All-Hands automatically.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or
higher, the effect additionally suppresses a target's desire to ask
clarifying questions during the live Q&A portion. Written or anonymous
questions submitted afterward are unaffected.
