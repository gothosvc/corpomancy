# Proxy Fight

*7th-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to file (a proxy statement), though the vote
itself is not held for 2 to 4 months<br>
**Range/Area:** Special; one organization in which you hold a qualifying
stake<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a competing slate of board nominees, consumed; a
proxy solicitation budget, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the vote is certified<br>
**Classes:** Activist Investor, Fund Manager, Board Nominee

Choose one organization in which you hold a qualifying stake and one or
more seats currently held on its board. Rather than persuading
shareholders to side with you informally, as with Activist Campaign, you
compel a formal vote on your nominees directly. Every shareholder of
record must be solicited; a shareholder who does not return a vote is
counted as voting with the incumbent board, regardless of their actual
opinion of either slate.

If your nominees receive a majority of votes cast, they replace the
incumbents holding the contested seats at the conclusion of the meeting,
with full authority effective immediately. If they do not, you may not
file another Proxy Fight against the same organization for one year,
though an active Activist Campaign is unaffected and may continue.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 8th level or
higher, an active Due Diligence finding may be published as part of your
solicitation materials, granting disadvantage on the incumbent board's
collective Tenure saving throw for the duration of the vote.

**Flavor:** *"We urge shareholders to vote the WHITE card and reject the
incumbent board's continued mismanagement."* — solicitation mailer, one of
