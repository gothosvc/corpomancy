# Objection Handling

*2nd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a prospect raises a
concern about price, timeline, or fit
**Range/Area:** 30 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Account Executive, Sales Engineer

When a prospect voices a specific objection, you may respond immediately
with a reframing that neither confirms nor denies the substance of the
objection, but redirects the conversation toward a related benefit. The
prospect must succeed on a Tenure saving throw or treat the objection as
adequately addressed, despite it not having been answered.

If the same prospect raises the same objection a second time in the same
conversation, this spell is cast with disadvantage. A third repetition
fails automatically, and the objection must be handled honestly or the deal
is lost.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell's effectiveness does not scale with a
higher-level slot; casting it at a higher level changes nothing about the
outcome.
