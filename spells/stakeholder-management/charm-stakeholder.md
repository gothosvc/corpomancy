# Charm Stakeholder

*1st-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically disguised as "grabbing coffee"
**Range/Area:** 30 feet, or the length of a video call
**Components:** V, S (an enthusiastic tone and unbroken eye contact)
**Duration:** 1 hour, or until the target is presented with a competing
proposal, whichever comes first
**Classes:** Account Executive, Product Manager, anyone with a deck

Choose one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a
Tenure saving throw or regard your current proposal, initiative, or request
favorably for the duration, provided the request is not obviously against
the target's own interests. A charmed creature does the caster's proposal
proactive favors — championing it in meetings the caster is not present
for, allocating budget toward it — without recognizing this behavior as
unusual.

You know whether the spell succeeded or failed. A target who succeeds on
the saving throw is unaware an attempt was made, and may simply believe the
meeting went well.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, you may affect one additional stakeholder for each slot level above
1st, provided all targets are charmed during the same meeting.

follow-up email, sent nine minutes after the call ended
