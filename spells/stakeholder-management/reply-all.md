# Reply All

*3rd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, immediately regretted<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; every creature on the original recipient list,
and every creature subsequently added to it<br>
**Components:** V (a message intended for a smaller subset of the
eventual recipients)<br>
**Duration:** Until the thread reaches a creature with the authority to
mute it, or 3 business days, whichever comes first<br>
**Classes:** Anyone With an Email Client, New Hire, IT Administrator

You send a message to a distribution list larger than you intended,
revealing information — a confidential detail, an unfiltered opinion, a
reply meant for one person — to every creature on the list simultaneously.
Each recipient must succeed on a Tenure saving throw or feel compelled to
reply, publicly, to the entire list; a majority of these replies address
the distribution list itself rather than the original message ("please
remove me," "reply all," "+1").

Each reply is treated as a new casting of this spell, targeting the same
list, for as long as the duration lasts. No creature on the list may
unilaterally end the spell; it ends only when a creature with
mailing-list administrative authority is reached and chooses to act,
resolved as a Discovery saving throw made by the organization rather than
by any individual targeted creature.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, the original message may contain information otherwise protected
by a Nondisclosure agreement, in which case the spell additionally forces
a Discovery saving throw for the organization against its own
confidentiality obligations.
