# Poach

*3rd-level Sales & Stakeholder Management*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically disguised as "grabbing coffee"<br>
**Range/Area:** 30 feet, or the length of a phone call; one employee of a
rival organization you can identify as a competitor<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an offer letter with a number circled twice,
consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** Hiring Manager, VP of Talent, Founder

Choose one employee of a rival organization. That creature must succeed on
a Tenure saving throw, made with disadvantage if their current organization
has cast Reduction in Force, Burn Rate, or All-Hands against them more than
once in the past year. On a failed save, the creature accepts your offer
and Converts into your organization's employee at the start of their next
pay period.

The target organization does not automatically learn this spell was cast
against them; a Bus Factor already in effect on the departing creature
triggers normally regardless.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, you may target one additional employee of the same rival
organization, provided both accept within the same conversation (a Team
Lift-Out).
