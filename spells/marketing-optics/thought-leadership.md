# Thought Leadership

*1st-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, repeatable indefinitely
**Range/Area:** Self
**Components:** S, M (a professional networking profile, a conference
badge, or a podcast microphone, not consumed)
**Duration:** Until the caster stops posting, or is asked a follow-up
question requiring genuine expertise, whichever comes first
**Classes:** Founder, VP, anyone with an audience

You cause yourself to appear, to any creature reading, watching, or
listening to you, as an authority on a subject of your choosing. This
perception is independent of your actual credentials, track record, or
depth of knowledge on that subject.

The illusion persists under passive engagement — reading a post, listening
to a talk — but is dispelled immediately for any creature who engages you
directly with a specific, technical follow-up question, unless you succeed
on a check to redirect the conversation to a related but safer topic.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, creatures affected by an earlier casting become more likely to
amplify future castings without being separately targeted, at no additional
cost to you.

wrong. Here's what I've learned after one project."* — networking post,
