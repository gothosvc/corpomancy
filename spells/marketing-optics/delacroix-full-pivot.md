# Delacroix's Full Pivot

*6th-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute to announce, though the underlying
repositioning takes 6 to 18 months, same as Rebrand<br>
**Range/Area:** Self, extending to affect perception of the organization's
entire market category<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (Rebrand, expended as a component; a new company
name, ticker symbol, and founding story)<br>
**Duration:** Until the next Full Pivot<br>
**Classes:** CEO, CMO, Board

This spell functions as Rebrand, except the illusion extends to the
organization's origin, not just its current appearance. Any creature who
recalls the organization's previous name, product, or founding story must
succeed on a Discovery saving throw or find the memory increasingly
difficult to substantiate publicly — search results, press archives, and
former colleagues all become, independently and without coordination,
harder to locate.

A creature who succeeds on the saving throw retains full and specific
knowledge of the organization's history, but finds no forum in which
stating it is considered relevant to the conversation at hand.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 7th level or
higher, the new founding story may include a claim of having invented the
market category the organization previously entered as a late competitor.

---
**Flavor:** *"We were never really a [previous company]. We've always been
building toward this."* — founder interview, three weeks after the
S-1 amendment
