# Greenwash

*2nd-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range/Area:** Self, or one product or practice you designate
**Components:** V, S, M (a stock photo of a forest, or a leaf icon)
**Duration:** 1 year, or until an independent investigation succeeds
against it
**Classes:** CMO, Head of Sustainability, VP of Communications

You cause a product, practice, or organization to appear environmentally
responsible to any creature evaluating it through ordinary means —
marketing materials, packaging, a company website. This perception is
entirely cosmetic: the spell has no effect on the actual environmental
impact of the affected subject.

A creature that investigates the subject directly — auditing its supply
chain, testing its claims, requesting underlying data — may attempt a
Discovery saving throw against the spell; your organization has
disadvantage on this save if the underlying practice is significantly
harmful.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, the spell additionally generates a published sustainability report,
which is treated as Documented (see Animate Legacy System) for the purposes
of any effect requiring supporting evidence, whether or not the underlying
data supports its conclusions.
