# Marketing & Optics

*Classical tradition: Illusion*

Field notes on Illusion as practiced here: spin, narrative, misdirection —
brand, PR, and the specific art of making a number or a decision look
better than it is. Not marketing broadly (see `SCHOOLS.md`'s Provisional
list for that); this discipline is specifically the deceptive,
optics-management angle. It is also, we suspect, the discipline most
likely to eventually be practiced on us.

**Field tone:** glossy, evasive, technically-not-lying. The caster changes
how something is perceived, not what it is.

**Vocabulary recorded in the field:** narrative control, messaging, brand
refresh, spin, optics, greenwashing, talking points, reframe, on-message,
investor deck, soft launch, thought leadership, "let me be clear" (they
will not be clear), "available," focus time, async-first, out of office
(not actually out of office).
