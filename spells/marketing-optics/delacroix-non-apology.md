# Delacroix's Non-Apology

*2nd-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, drafted by legal before it is read by the
caster<br>
**Range/Area:** One publicized incident<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a statement containing the phrase "mistakes were
made," consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** CEO, Communications Lead, General Counsel

You issue a statement, shaped like an apology, that concedes no fault and
commits to no specific remedy. Any creature who hears or reads the
statement perceives it as contrition, though it grants no advantage to a
later Discovery saving throw attempting to establish who was actually
responsible for the incident — the statement's language is constructed to
be equally useless as evidence in either direction.

This spell has no effect on the underlying incident, its cause, or its
consequences, and does not by itself grant the Documented status to
anything the statement contains.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, casting this spell within 24 hours of the precipitating event
reduces the casting time of a subsequent Crisis Management spell targeting
the same incident to 1 action, with no lead time required.

**Flavor:** *"We hold ourselves to the highest standards, and we take full
accountability for ensuring this conversation continues."* — statement,
issued once
