# Power Word: On-Brand

*2nd-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, or a single word muttered in a design
review<br>
**Range/Area:** Sight; one creative asset, message, or decision under
review<br>
**Components:** V ("brand")<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the veto is not revisited<br>
**Classes:** CMO, VP of Brand, Anyone With Final Sign-Off

This spell requires no saving throw. Its effect is gated entirely on
whether the caster holds final sign-off authority over brand — not on
whether the targeted work is actually inconsistent with any documented
guideline. A caster who holds that authority may veto or approve the
target purely by invoking this word; a caster who does not hold it may say
the same word with no effect whatsoever.

No brand guideline needs to be cited, consulted, or shown to exist for
this spell to succeed. If one is later produced to justify the casting
after the fact, it is frequently written specifically to match the
decision already made.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot; the guidelines do not get any more real.

---
**Flavor:** *"This just doesn't feel very on-brand to me."* — sole
feedback, design review, six weeks of work
