# Narrative Control

*7th-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, cast before a known negative event becomes
public rather than after (see Crisis Management for the reactive
version)<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an exclusive story, offered to a friendly outlet
ahead of the news it's meant to blunt; an embargo agreement, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the embargoed event breaks independently, or 1 week,
whichever is shorter<br>
**Classes:** CMO, Communications Lead, CEO

Choose one event you know is likely to become public and unfavorable to
your organization within the duration. You control which outlet, and in
what framing, the event is first reported. For the duration, any creature
attempting to establish an independent account of what happened has
disadvantage on the Discovery saving throw, for as long as your version
remains the only one in circulation.

This spell has no effect once the event has already become public through
a source you don't control; at that point, only Crisis Management can be
cast against it.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 8th level or
higher, you may cast this spell as part of the same action as Crisis
Management targeting the same event, gaining the benefits of both without
needing to cast Crisis Management within 24 hours of the event as its
casting time normally requires.

---
**Flavor:** *"We wanted to get ahead of this story and make sure the full
context was represented accurately."* — background quote, granted to one
reporter, thirty minutes before the story broke everywhere else
