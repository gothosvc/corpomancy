# Astroturfing

*5th-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though the appearance of organic growth takes
2 to 6 weeks to seed convincingly<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; any public forum, review platform, or social
media conversation about your organization<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a network of accounts, personas, or paid
participants, consumed; a sentiment to be seeded)<br>
**Duration:** Until a creature makes a deliberate effort to trace the
sentiment's origin<br>
**Classes:** Communications Lead, Growth Marketing Manager, PR Agency (as
a ritual, at considerable cost)

You create the appearance of independent, organic public support for your
organization, product, or position. Any creature encountering the
resulting reviews, comments, or coverage perceives it as a genuine
grassroots reaction unless they succeed on a Discovery saving throw
specifically directed at the sentiment's *origin* — scrutiny directed at
its content instead, however rigorous, grants no benefit against this
spell.

If a creature succeeds on this saving throw, the deception becomes obvious
to any other creature it's revealed to, and this spell cannot be recast
targeting the same forum for the same underlying event.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or
higher, this spell can additionally sway a public sentiment metric tracked
by an Analytics & Forecasting spell, or a Halloran's Read of the Room
consensus, as though the sentiment were organic.
