# Work From Beach Home

*2nd-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, recast at the start of each work day<br>
**Range/Area:** Self, observed at Slack-status range<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a green "active" dot, a calendar blocked "Focus
Time")<br>
**Duration:** Concentration, up to one fiscal quarter<br>
**Classes:** C-Suite, VP, Director

You become illusorily present to any creature observing you through
ordinary indirect means — status indicators, calendar density, an email
sent at a conspicuously late hour. Actual availability is unaffected by
this spell in either direction.

A creature attempting to reach you directly — scheduling a call, requesting
a decision — must succeed on a Discovery saving throw to determine your
true availability before committing time to the attempt. Success on this
save does not guarantee a response.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, you may additionally appear at one all-hands meeting via
pre-recorded video without breaking concentration on the rest of the
spell.
