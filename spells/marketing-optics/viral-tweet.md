# Viral Tweet

*3rd-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, and frequently cast without meaning to<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited<br>
**Components:** V only (a single public post, unedited)<br>
**Duration:** 1d4 days, and cannot be shortened by any means available to
the caster<br>
**Classes:** Social Media Manager, Founder, CEO

You cause a single public statement to escape your control. Its content
is fixed at the moment of casting, but its effect is not: at the end of
the first hour, determine at random whether the spell deals damage to
your own organization — identical to an unprompted Smear Campaign cast by
an unknown party — or grants your organization a Windfall of public
goodwill, in proportion to nothing about the statement's actual merit.

You cannot end this spell early. Deleting, editing, or issuing a
clarification does not dispel it; any such attempt instead grants a
Discovery saving throw, with advantage, to every creature who saw the
original statement, and a failed save on your part extends the spell's
duration rather than ending it.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may name one additional creature or organization mentioned in
the original statement. That target independently rolls its own random
effect, unconnected to yours.

---
**Flavor:** *"Can't believe this blew up like this, just trying to start
a conversation!"* — reply, posted six hours after the original, to no
effect
