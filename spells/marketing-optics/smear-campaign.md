# Smear Campaign

*4th-level Marketing & Optics*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one organization you can identify as a
competitor
**Components:** V, S, M (an anonymous tip, or a strategically timed leak,
consumed)
**Duration:** Until the news cycle moves on, typically 1d4 weeks
**Classes:** CMO, Head of Communications, outside PR firm (as a ritual)

Choose a rival organization. You seed a piece of true, exaggerated, or
misleading information about them into public circulation, without the
source being traceable to you. The target must succeed on a Discovery
saving throw or suffer a reduction in Blamelessness score and a
corresponding drop in stakeholder confidence, treated as though it had
recently failed a Root Cause Analysis publicly.

If the underlying information was entirely fabricated rather than merely
exaggerated, this spell still functions, but your own organization becomes
subject to a Discovery saving throw of its own, made by any creature
investigating the leak's origin.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th
level or higher, the target has disadvantage on its Discovery saving throw
if your organization has cast Greenwash or Rebrand within the past year —
the target has less standing credibility to push back with.

actually run over there — obviously can't say more than that."* —
