# Power Word: Blocked

*1st-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically cast during standup<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V (the word "blocked," followed by a dependency named
just specifically enough to sound real)<br>
**Duration:** Until the next standup, or until someone with the authority
to check follows up, whichever is later<br>
**Classes:** Engineer, Anyone With a Ticket Assigned to Them

This spell requires no saving throw. Its effect is gated on whether the
named dependency is real: if it is, you were already blocked, and this
spell changes nothing. If it isn't, this spell functions perfectly
anyway, absolving you of the ticket's lack of progress for the duration —
and no creature present, including your manager, is in a position to tell
the two outcomes apart without independently verifying the dependency,
which essentially never happens inside the duration.

This spell does not require you to have attempted to resolve the blocker
before casting it.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot; a more senior engineer is not blocked more
convincingly.
