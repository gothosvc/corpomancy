# System Sunset

*7th-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 8 hours (a Migration), preceded by a mandatory Freeze
period of 1 week<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one Legacy Servant<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a fully staffed replacement system, functioning
and consumed in the casting; a signed executive sponsor sign-off, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; permanent<br>
**Classes:** VP of Engineering, Director of Engineering, executive sponsor

Touch one Legacy Servant. Its On-Call Engineer is released from their
permanent assignment, and the Legacy Servant is destroyed. Unlike the
damage dealt when a Legacy Servant's On-Call Engineer merely becomes
unavailable, this destruction is controlled: no damage is dealt to any
other system, provided the replacement system component was genuinely
functioning at the time of casting.

If the replacement system was not genuinely functioning — a state this
spell cannot verify, only assume — the Legacy Servant is destroyed anyway,
and every system that depended on it takes damage equal to the combined
Technical Debt score of every Legacy Servant the caster's organization
currently maintains.

No other spell can permanently end a Legacy Servant's duration. Simply
allowing an On-Call Engineer to become unavailable only ends the spell that
raised the Legacy Servant — it does not end the Legacy Servant itself, so
much as pass its damage on unresolved.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th
level or higher, you may target one additional Legacy Servant for each
slot level above 7th, provided they share a replacement system.
