# Add New Column

*2nd-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 action; the actual consequences may not surface for
weeks<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch; one spreadsheet or workbook currently relied upon
by the caster's organization<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a single inserted, deleted, or moved column,
row, or tab)<br>
**Duration:** Permanent, until independently discovered<br>
**Classes:** Financial Analyst, Operations Manager, New Hire

You insert, delete, or move a single column, row, or tab in a target
spreadsheet. Every formula referencing a cell at or past the point of
change silently shifts to reference the wrong value; no error is
produced, because the file's underlying formula chain has never been
documented and no creature currently employed understands its full
structure. This is a precondition for the spell, not a coincidence.

Any creature who would rely on the file's output before the change is
discovered must succeed on a Discovery saving throw, its DC set by the
file's Technical Debt score (see Animate Legacy System), to notice the
discrepancy before acting on it. On a failure, the organization takes
damage equal to the scale of whatever decision was made using the bad
number — this damage cannot be reduced, resisted, or prepared for in
advance, even by a creature who suspected this exact spell had been cast.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, the shift additionally propagates to any other file that has ever
copied a formula from this one, without requiring a separate casting.
