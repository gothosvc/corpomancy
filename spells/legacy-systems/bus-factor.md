# Bus Factor

*2nd-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically cast unintentionally during a
performance review or team restructuring
**Range/Area:** Touch, one creature and one system
**Components:** V, S, M (an org chart listing exactly one name under
"Owner")
**Duration:** Until the designated creature becomes unavailable for any
reason
**Classes:** Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering, whoever drew the
original org chart

You designate one willing or unwilling creature as the sole point of
failure for a target system. For the duration, the designated creature
becomes remarkably effective at maintaining the system, gaining advantage
on all checks made to operate, extend, or repair it. The system itself
gains a measure of stability it did not previously have.

This stability is not real. If the designated creature becomes unavailable
for any reason — resignation, extended leave, promotion out of the role, or
otherwise — the spell ends immediately, and the system takes damage equal
to all the institutional knowledge that was never documented while the
spell was active. This damage cannot be reduced, resisted, or prepared for
in advance, even by a creature who suspected this might happen.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd
level or higher, you may designate one additional creature as a secondary
point of failure. This does not reduce the damage dealt when the primary
designated creature becomes unavailable; it only means the system takes the
same damage again, later, when the second creature also leaves.
