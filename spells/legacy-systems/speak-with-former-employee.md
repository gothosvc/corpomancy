# Speak with Former Employee

*3rd-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute<br>
**Range/Area:** Special (requires a phone number, personal email, or mutual
professional connection previously established)<br>
**Components:** S, M (a severance agreement, referenced but not consumed)<br>
**Duration:** 10 minutes, or until the recipient stops responding<br>
**Classes:** Engineering Manager, HR Business Partner, Product Manager

You contact a former employee of a target organization and may ask them up
to five questions concerning their former role, decisions they made, or
systems they built. The former employee answers to the best of their
willingness, which is affected by how the organization is currently
believed to have treated them: a former employee who departed on good terms
answers helpfully and in detail; one who was affected by a casting of
Reduction in Force answers evasively, incompletely, or declines to help
without payment.

This spell cannot compel information the former employee never possessed,
and cannot compel disclosure of anything covered by a non-disparagement
agreement they signed on the way out.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, you may contact one additional former employee for each
slot level above 3rd, and may offer a consulting fee to bypass the
non-disparagement restriction for any one of them.
