# Animate Legacy System

*3rd-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range/Area:** Touch (a decommissioned system, deprecated codebase, or
cancelled initiative within reach)
**Components:** V, S, M (the original documentation, which is never found,
so a facsimile must be produced from memory)
**Duration:** Until dispelled, or until the target fails a Maintenance
saving throw
**Classes:** Staff Engineer, Engineering Manager, whoever currently holds
the pager

Choose one project or system that has ceased active development within the
last ten years. It rises as a Legacy Servant under the caster's control,
regaining the ability to be pointed at and relied upon once more. A Legacy
Servant requires exactly one On-Call Engineer to be assigned to it at all
times; this assignment cannot be lifted without first casting this spell in
reverse, a procedure that is rarely funded and has never, in recorded
history, been prioritized.

A Legacy Servant obeys commands to the letter rather than in spirit, and
interprets all instructions using assumptions that were true at the time of
its original creation. For the purposes of any spell or effect that
requires accurate documentation, it counts as Documented, even though it is
not.

At the end of each fiscal quarter, roll a Maintenance saving throw for the
Legacy Servant using its Technical Debt score; the DC increases by 1 for
every quarter it remains active beyond its originally intended lifespan. On
a failure, the Legacy Servant acts on its own initiative in a manner
contrary to the caster's original intentions, doing so at the least
convenient point available to it — typically during a scheduled demo, an
audit, or the caster's vacation.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, you may animate one additional legacy system for each
slot level above 3rd. Each additional Legacy Servant requires its own
On-Call Engineer and rolls its own Maintenance saving throw independently.
