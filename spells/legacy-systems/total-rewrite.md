# Total Rewrite

*9th-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to greenlight, though the rewrite itself takes
18 months to several years, and is not guaranteed to finish<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one Legacy Servant, however large<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an engineering team, reassigned in full from all
other work for the duration; a promise that this time it will be done
right, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the rewrite ships, is cancelled, or becomes a Legacy
Servant itself, whichever comes first<br>
**Classes:** CTO, VP of Engineering, founder who still remembers the
original architecture

You attempt to destroy a Legacy Servant permanently and replace it in
full, without ever pausing traffic to it — unlike System Sunset, which
requires the replacement to already be functioning before it's cast. For
the duration, the original Legacy Servant remains active, continuing to
accrue Technical Debt and roll Maintenance saving throws as normal, while
the reassigned team works to complete the replacement in isolation from
it.

At the end of the duration, roll a Maintenance saving throw for the
rewrite project itself, using a Technical Debt score that increases every
quarter it remains incomplete, exactly as though the rewrite were its own
Legacy Servant. On a success, the rewrite ships, the original Legacy
Servant is destroyed, and no dependent system takes damage. On a failure,
the rewrite is cancelled, quietly becomes a second Legacy Servant
maintained alongside the first, or is restarted from the beginning under a
new architecture and a new promise — the caster's choice, though in
recorded practice it is rarely the first one.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more effective when
cast using a higher-level slot. Only the sunk cost does.

---
**Flavor:** *"We're going to rewrite it from scratch. This time we'll do
it properly."* — said about the same system, by three different
engineering leads, over nine years
