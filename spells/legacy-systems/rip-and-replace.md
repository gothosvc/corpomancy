# Rip and Replace

*5th-level Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to initiate, though full effect takes 1d4
months (a Migration Incentive)<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one rival organization's installed customer
base, where the rival is one you can identify as a competitor<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a competitive pricing offer, consumed; a data
migration tool, not consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the migration window closes, commonly 90 days<br>
**Classes:** VP of Sales, Head of Growth

Choose a rival organization's product or system. You offer their existing
customers a forcible off-ramp — discounted pricing, automated data
migration, a dedicated onboarding team — designed to make switching to your
product easier than remaining with theirs. Each affected customer must
succeed on a Tenure saving throw, representing their institutional inertia
and switching costs, or migrate to your organization at the end of the
duration.

If the rival's product is itself a Legacy Servant with an unresolved
Maintenance failure on record, this spell is cast with advantage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th
level or higher, customers who fail their saving throw arrive already
partially onboarded, skipping the disadvantage a Contractor or conjured
employee would normally suffer during Onboarding.

**Flavor:** *"Switch today and we'll cover 100% of your migration costs —
no questions asked."* — landing page, competitor's brand name conspicuously
absent
