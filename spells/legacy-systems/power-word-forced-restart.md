# Power Word: Forced Restart

*Legacy Systems & Zombie Projects cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one malfunctioning system<br>
**Components:** V (two words, spoken with escalating certainty: "did
you—")<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** On-Call Engineer, IT Support, Whoever Is Standing Nearby

This spell requires no saving throw and no diagnosis. If the target
system has not yet failed its most recent Maintenance saving throw (see
Animate Legacy System), its malfunction ends immediately, its symptoms
resolved without its underlying cause being identified, understood, or
documented by anyone present.

If the system has already failed that Maintenance check, this spell has
no effect — though it may still be attempted as many times as the
caster's patience allows before anyone concludes otherwise. This spell's
success rate is high enough that no organization employing it has ever
felt compelled to investigate why.

**Flavor:** *"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"* — On-Call
Engineer, correctly, for the fourth time this month
