# Dashboard

*1st-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes to set up, 1 action to consult thereafter<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited, so long as the underlying data pipeline remains
connected<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a login credential, not consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 8 hours (commonly re-cast every morning)<br>
**Classes:** Data Analyst, Product Manager, anyone with a login

You create an invisible sensor that displays a curated selection of your
organization's metrics, updated on a delay of no less than 24 hours. While
concentrating, you may consult the sensor as an action to learn the current
value of up to six metrics you selected at the time of casting.

The sensor only displays metrics you thought, at the time of casting, would
be worth tracking. It cannot answer questions about a metric you did not
think to include, and will not proactively inform you if a more important
one has emerged elsewhere.

A creature that stares at the sensor long enough begins to mistake the
metrics displayed for the entirety of the underlying reality; treat this
creature as under an effect identical to nondetection, except that it
applies only to whatever the sensor does not measure.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, the delay decreases to 1 hour and you may track two additional
metrics for each slot level above 1st. Each additional metric increases the
DC of any check made to understand what any of them actually mean.
