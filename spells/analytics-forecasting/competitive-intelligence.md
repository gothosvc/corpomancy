# Competitive Intelligence

*2nd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one organization you can identify, or wish to
identify, as a competitor
**Components:** V, S, M (a LinkedIn search history, a leaked pricing
sheet, or a former employee's memory)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
**Classes:** Data Analyst, VP of Strategy, Product Marketing Manager

You divine a curated picture of a rival organization's internal metrics,
headcount, and strategic priorities, accurate as of some point in the past
you cannot precisely determine. While concentrating, you may ask up to
three questions about the target, answered with the same reliability as a
Dashboard — confidently, and only about whatever the target chose to make
visible in the first place.

Casting this spell is what formally qualifies a rival organization as one
you "can identify as a competitor" for the purposes of any other spell in
this collection that requires it, if you did not already qualify.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd
level or higher, you may additionally cast Speak with Former Employee
against a former employee of the target as part of this same casting,
without needing a separate pre-existing connection to them.
