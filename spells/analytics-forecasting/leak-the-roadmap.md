# Leak the Roadmap

*3rd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though it requires the target to already be
under the effect of Competitive Intelligence<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one organization you can identify as a
competitor, currently revealed to you by your own casting of Competitive
Intelligence<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an anonymous account, a screenshot with the
metadata still attached)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** CEO, CMO, VP of Strategy

Choose a rival organization currently revealed to you by an active
casting of Competitive Intelligence. You disclose one piece of information
gained from that casting to a party of your choosing — the press, an
investor, a mutual customer, the target's own employees. The target
organization must succeed on a Discovery saving throw to trace the leak
back to you before the information causes it to act.

On a failed save, the target is compelled to react to the leaked
information immediately, whether or not doing so is actually in its
interest — rushing a launch, issuing a denial, restructuring around a plan
that was never confirmed. On a successful save, the target correctly
traces the leak back to you instead, and gains a Windfall of political
capital, spendable to cast an offensive spell of its own against you at no
cost.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, you may leak the information to multiple parties
simultaneously.

---
**Flavor:** *"According to sources familiar with the matter..."* — trade
publication, story published four hours after the anonymous tip, never
independently confirmed
