# Halloran's Confidence Interval

*4th-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a range of values, stated with a precision the
underlying data does not support)<br>
**Duration:** Until the actual result falls outside the stated range<br>
**Classes:** VP of Strategy, Director of Data, Analyst

You state a numerical estimate bounded by an upper and lower value. For
the duration, the estimate is treated as accurate regardless of the true
result, so long as the true result falls within the stated bounds. If the
true result falls outside the bounds, this spell does not end in failure;
instead, you may immediately recast it with the range widened just enough
to contain the true result, at no additional cost and without expending an
additional spell slot. If you have an active Halloran Principle, you may
instead spend 1 point of Conviction to recast it this way even after the
normal window for a free recast has closed.

This spell can be recast in this manner an unlimited number of times. Each
recasting is presented as a refinement of the previous estimate, never as
a correction of it.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or
higher, the recast range may be stated in a unit not previously disclosed
(e.g. switching from absolute figures to percentage variance), further
obscuring how far the original estimate had to move.

**Flavor:** *"We're now forecasting a range of $180-220M, down from our
original guidance of $240-260M, reflecting continued confidence in our
core business."* — investor letter
