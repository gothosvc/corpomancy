# Power Word: Directionally Correct

*2nd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, cast the moment someone asks for the exact
number<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V (a number, spoken with confidence disproportionate to
how it was derived)<br>
**Duration:** Until someone with access to the underlying data checks<br>
**Classes:** Data Analyst, VP of Strategy, Product Manager

This spell requires no saving throw. Its effect is gated on whether the
stated number has the correct sign and order of magnitude — not on
whether it is actually accurate. A number that is up when it should be
up, by roughly the right amount, satisfies this spell completely,
regardless of the precision anyone believed they were being given.

A creature that later checks the underlying data and finds the number
wrong in magnitude, not just precision, may retroactively succeed on a
Discovery saving throw against this casting — though by then the number
has usually already been used in a decision.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, you may cast this on a range rather than a single number,
expanding what counts as "directionally correct" to include nearly any
outcome short of the opposite sign.

---
**Flavor:** *"It's directionally correct, we can tighten it up later."*
— said in every meeting where "later" is not on the agenda
