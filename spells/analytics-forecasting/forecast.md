# Forecast

*2nd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, typically performed at the start of a fiscal
quarter
**Range/Area:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a spreadsheet containing at least one assumption
labeled "TBD")
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Data Analyst, Finance Business Partner, VP of Strategy

You divine a general omen about the outcome of a course of action your
organization is about to undertake within the next quarter. The response is
Ahead of Plan, On Track, or Behind Plan. The casting has no bearing on
whether the outcome will actually occur; it reports the caster's confidence
at the moment of casting, not a prediction of the future.

If you cast this spell more than once regarding the same course of action
within the same fiscal quarter, each subsequent casting is 10 percent more
likely to simply return whatever answer the caster wanted to hear.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd
level or higher, the divination includes a specific numeric range (a
Confidence Interval), which sounds more precise than the 2nd-level version
without being meaningfully more accurate.

business review, slide last updated the week before the miss was announced
