# The Algorithm

*9th-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a training dataset comprising the entirety of
your organization's historical data, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Permanent, until the underlying data distribution shifts — a
condition this spell cannot detect in itself<br>
**Classes:** VP of Data, Chief Data Officer

You create a permanent, standing divination that answers any question
about your organization's future performance with near-perfect accuracy,
provided the question concerns a pattern that has held true in your
organization's past. Once created, this spell requires no further action to
consult: you simply know the answer, the way you know your own name.

This spell cannot answer any question about a genuinely novel situation,
and — critically — cannot tell you when a situation has become genuinely
novel. Its answers remain confident, precise, and wrong for an
unpredictable period of time before anyone notices, during which every
decision made using this spell compounds the error.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell does not become more accurate at higher
levels; it only becomes more trusted.

**Flavor:** *"The model has a strong track record. We don't see any reason
that would change."* — internal memo, dated the week before it did
