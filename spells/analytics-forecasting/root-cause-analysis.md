# Root Cause Analysis

*3rd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour, conducted as a meeting with all relevant parties
present, or as many as could be scheduled on short notice
**Range/Area:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (an incident timeline, incomplete)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Engineering Manager, Site Reliability Engineer, VP of
Engineering

Following an incident, you attempt to divine its true, underlying cause,
rolling a check against your organization's Blamelessness score. On a high
enough result, the spell correctly identifies a systemic or process-level
cause, along with an actionable remediation. On a failure, it instead
identifies a single individual or team as the cause, regardless of whether
a systemic cause also exists; that creature suffers a lasting penalty to
standing the caster did not intend and will later describe as unfortunate.

This spell's accuracy is inversely proportional to how recently the
organization's leadership was involved in decisions related to the
incident.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may compel the meeting's attendees to remain until an action
item is agreed upon. Nothing in this spell compels anyone to complete it
afterward.
