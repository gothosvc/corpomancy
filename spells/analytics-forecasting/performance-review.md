# Performance Review

*3rd-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour, though the outcome was substantively determined
before the meeting began<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one employee<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a rubric, and a self-assessment the target
wrote, largely disregarded)<br>
**Duration:** 1 year, or until the next Performance Review<br>
**Classes:** Manager, Director, HR Business Partner

You divine a rating for one creature's value to the organization over the
past review period. The result is rendered with complete confidence and is
accurate only by coincidence: it is driven primarily by the
organization's current headcount budget and the caster's memory of the
creature's most recent, most visible action, rather than by the full
period under review.

A creature who receives a low rating becomes Documented (see Animate
Legacy System) for the purposes of any future Reduction in Force or
Litigation Blitz targeting them specifically, regardless of whether the
rating reflected their actual output. Any Discovery saving throw the
creature attempts to formally dispute the rating is made with
disadvantage, since the rubric used to produce it was never shared in
advance.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may cast this spell on an entire team simultaneously, forcing
a fixed rating distribution across the team regardless of its actual
relative performance.

but we're excited about where this is headed."* — review document, same
wording as the previous three years, rating: Meets Some Expectations
