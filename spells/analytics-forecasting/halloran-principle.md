# The Halloran Principle

*5th-level Analytics & Forecasting*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S<br>
**Duration:** 1 year<br>
**Classes:** VP of Strategy, Director of Data, Analyst

For the duration, you gain a pool of Conviction equal to your level, which
refills to full at the start of each fiscal quarter regardless of whether
the Conviction you spent last quarter was later vindicated or discredited.
Any forecast, estimate, or prediction you state gains the Documented
status automatically. If a creature attempts a Discovery saving throw to
verify one of your forecasts after the fact, you may spend 1 point of
Conviction to force that saving throw to automatically fail.

The strength of this effect is set by your seniority at the time of
casting, not by the accuracy of any forecast you have previously made — a
target correct once in ten attempts spends Conviction identically to one
correct nine times in ten, provided their level is the same. A creature
affected by this spell who is later proven wrong suffers no penalty to
their remaining Conviction, current or future.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or
higher, your Conviction is treated as though it applies to any forecast
issued by your organization bearing your name, whether or not you were
personally consulted before it was issued.
