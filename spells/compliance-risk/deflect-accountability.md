# Deflect Accountability

*3rd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, taken when the caster or their initiative
would be blamed for a failure<br>
**Range/Area:** Self, or one direct report within earshot<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a reorg chart, or a retro doc vague enough to
survive scrutiny)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the redirected blame persists for the life of
institutional memory<br>
**Classes:** Director, VP, C-Suite

When triggered, this spell reduces the effective Blamelessness score of
the next Root Cause Analysis conducted on the triggering failure,
guaranteeing that check identifies an individual cause rather than a
systemic one. The caster designates who.

This spell has no effect on the underlying cause of the failure, which
remains exactly as systemic as it was before casting.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, the designated target may be a team or function rather than an
individual creature, at the caster's discretion.
