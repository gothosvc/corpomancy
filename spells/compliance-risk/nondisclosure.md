# Nondisclosure

*1st-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a signed agreement and a one-time payment of
consideration, both consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the underlying agreement expires; many are drafted to
never expire<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, HR Business Partner, Compliance Officer

Touch a willing or coerced creature. That creature is bound from speaking,
writing, or otherwise disclosing information about a specific event,
agreement, or period of employment that you specify at the time of casting.
The binding persists regardless of the creature's location, employment
status, or subsequent grievances.

A creature attempting to violate this binding must succeed on a Tenure
saving throw or immediately suffer legal and reputational consequences
equal to the value of the original consideration paid, plus interest. On a
success, the creature may speak freely, but forfeits any remaining
consideration owed under the agreement.

This spell does not prevent the bound creature from disclosing information
to a licensed attorney, a government regulator, or law enforcement, though
the bound creature is rarely informed of this exception at the time of
signing.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd
level or higher, the binding additionally prevents any negative
characterization of your organization, factual or not, rather than just
disclosure of the specified information.
