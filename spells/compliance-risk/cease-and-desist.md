# Cease and Desist

*2nd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** 60 feet, or the reach of a process server; one creature or
organization you can identify as infringing, competing, or otherwise
interfering<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a letter on law firm letterhead, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the target is bound for 30 days or until the
underlying dispute is resolved<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, outside counsel

Choose one creature or organization within range. Make a ranged attack
against their Solvency; on a hit, the target must immediately halt the
specific action you named at the time of casting or begin accumulating
legal exposure equal to 2d6 per day the action continues.

This spell deals no damage on its own — it is a threat, not a strike — but
a target who calls your bluff by continuing the named action anyway forces
you to follow through with actual litigation or lose all credibility for
future castings of this spell against the same target. A target may
respond immediately with Indemnification if they believe the underlying
claim is meritless.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd
level or higher, you may name an additional specific action to halt, and
the accumulating exposure increases to 3d6 per day.
