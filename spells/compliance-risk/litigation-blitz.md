# Litigation Blitz

*5th-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to file, though the underlying case is not
resolved for 1d4 years<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one organization you can identify as a
competitor<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a complaint, filed; a legal budget, not
consumed, expended continuously for the duration)<br>
**Duration:** Until one party's Runway is exhausted or a settlement is
reached, whichever comes first<br>
**Classes:** CEO, General Counsel, Board

Unlike Cease and Desist, this spell requires no bluff to back it up — you
have already committed to litigating. Each round (year) of the spell's
duration, you and the target organization each take 2d6 Amortized Loss
damage from ongoing legal costs, applied regardless of the underlying
merit of the claim. At the end of the duration, whichever organization has
more Runway remaining is treated as having won, regardless of which
organization's claim was actually correct.

The target organization may end the spell early by attempting a Solvency
saving throw at the start of any round; on a success, it offers a
settlement, ending the spell for both parties but leaving the underlying
question legally unresolved either way.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th
level or higher, damage increases to 3d6 per round, reflecting appeals.

---
**Flavor:** *"The lawsuit could take years, but frankly, we can outlast
them."* — internal memo, forwarded without comment to outside counsel
