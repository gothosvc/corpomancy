# Personnel Matter

*4th-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, cast the moment a complaint is received rather
than at the moment of the underlying conduct<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch; one creature whose continued proximity to the
complainant would otherwise be unavoidable<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a signed separation or transfer agreement,
consumed; a severance or continued-compensation payment scaled to
seniority rather than to the underlying conduct)<br>
**Duration:** Permanent, so long as the resulting Nondisclosure holds<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, Chief HR Officer, Board

Touch the higher-seniority creature involved in a workplace complaint,
typically following an inconclusive result from Summon Human Resources.
That creature must succeed on a Tenure saving throw. On a success, they
are laterally reassigned to a different team, office, or reporting line
with no material loss of title or compensation, and you simultaneously
cast Nondisclosure on the complainant. On a failure — ordinarily only when
the creature's Tenure is unusually low for their conduct's severity —
they are separated from the organization instead, still by mutual
agreement, still with severance, and still under an identical
Nondisclosure.

Either outcome is recorded internally as resolved. For the duration, any
Discovery saving throw attempted against the underlying complaint — by a
journalist, a subsequent complainant establishing a pattern, a plaintiff's
attorney — automatically fails unless the Nondisclosure component is
independently broken.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or
higher, you may apply this spell to more than one prior complaint
concerning the same creature, consolidating them into a single
Nondisclosure and a single payment.

**Flavor:** *"They've decided to pursue other opportunities. We're

permitted to discuss
