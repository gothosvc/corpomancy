# Document Retention

*2nd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one document, policy, or record<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a folder containing no fewer than six versions of
the same file, none labeled as final, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until dispelled, or until the applicable retention period
lapses (commonly seven years), whichever is later<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, Compliance Officer, Records Custodian

The touched document becomes warded against identification. For the
duration, any attempt to determine which existing copy is the authoritative
or final version — including attempts made using Analytics & Forecasting
magic — automatically fails unless the caster wishes otherwise. A creature
making such an attempt instead locates a plausible copy that is not the
authoritative version, and has no means of determining that it is not.

Whenever a creature initiates a formal attempt to compel disclosure of the
authoritative version, such as a legal discovery request, the caster's
organization must succeed on a Discovery saving throw or the true version is
revealed to that creature. The saving throw is made using the proficiency
bonus of whichever Records Custodian is currently assigned to the document;
if no Records Custodian can be identified, the document is treated as having
automatically failed the save.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd
level or higher, the ward extends to cover every document, policy, or record
related to the same underlying matter, rather than a single document, for
each slot level above 2nd. Additionally, the caster's organization has
advantage on all Discovery saving throws made to protect a warded record.
