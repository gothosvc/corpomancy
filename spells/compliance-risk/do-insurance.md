# D&O Insurance

*6th-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to bind, though it must be purchased before any
claim-triggering decision is made<br>
**Range/Area:** Self, extending to every current officer and director of
your organization<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an annual premium, consumed; a policy whose
exclusions nobody reads until a claim is filed)<br>
**Duration:** 1 year, renewable<br>
**Classes:** Board, CEO, General Counsel

For the duration, whenever an officer or director is personally named in a
claim arising from a decision made in that capacity, they may attempt a
Solvency saving throw with advantage, backed by the policy's limits rather
than their own personal assets. Unlike Indemnification, this protection
requires no other creature to have signed anything — it is purchased from
a third party who was never involved in the decision being covered.

Coverage does not apply to any act the insurer can characterize, after the
fact, as fraudulent, criminal, or undertaken for personal profit rather
than the organization's benefit. This determination is made by the
insurer, not the insured, and is typically made only once a claim large
enough to matter has actually been filed.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 7th level or
higher, coverage may be extended retroactively to a decision made before
the policy was bound, provided the claim itself is filed after.

---
**Flavor:** *"the policy shall not cover any Loss arising from any
Wrongful Act committed with actual dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal
purpose or intent"* — policy exclusions, page 41 of 43, discovered during
the claim process
