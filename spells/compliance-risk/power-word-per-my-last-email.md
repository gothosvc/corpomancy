# Power Word: Per My Last Email

*1st-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, or the reach of a reply-all<br>
**Components:** V (five words, appended to the top of a forwarded
thread)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the underlying point, once reasserted,
cannot be un-asserted<br>
**Classes:** Manager, Project Manager, Anyone Cc'd Correctly the First
Time

This spell requires no saving throw. Its effect is gated entirely on
whether a prior instance of the point being reasserted was actually sent,
in writing, to the same recipient. If it was, the recipient's obligation
to have acted on it is treated as established beyond dispute — regardless
of whether they read it, understood it, or had the standing to act on it
at the time. If no such instance exists, the spell fails completely, and
forwarding an old thread with this incantation attached only reveals that
it doesn't.

This spell deals no direct damage itself. It functions primarily as a
prerequisite: a creature targeted by it becomes Documented (see Animate
Legacy System) for the purposes of any later Performance Review or dispute
over who was responsible for a delay.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, you may cast this spell preemptively — sent the moment a request
is made rather than after it goes unanswered — in which case it functions
identically but is universally recognized as passive-aggressive rather
than merely aggressive.
