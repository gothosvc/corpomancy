# Bind Subordinate

*3rd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, cast the moment a direct report's name
appears on an internal transfer or promotion list<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a business justification for why this creature
"can't be spared right now")<br>
**Duration:** Until the caster is no longer the target's manager, by any
means<br>
**Classes:** Manager, Director, VP

You ward one direct report against a specific change to their
employment — an internal transfer, a promotion to a different team, an
external offer they were prepared to accept. The target must succeed on a
Tenure saving throw or the change is blocked, typically through a delay, a
"just one more quarter," or a counter-offer that resolves nothing about
the original reason they wanted to leave.

This spell functions identically whether the caster genuinely believes the
block serves the target's interest or is simply reluctant to lose a good
headcount line; the spell cannot distinguish between the two, and neither,
usually, can the target.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may cast this spell on an entire team at once, preemptively
blocking any transfer or promotion attempt for the duration without
needing to react to a specific triggering event.
