# Indemnification

*3rd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when you or your organization
would suffer liability, damage, or blame<br>
**Range/Area:** 30 feet, or the reach of a signed contract, whichever is
greater<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a contract containing an indemnification clause,
previously signed by the target)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the redirected consequence lasts for the life
of the underlying agreement<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, Risk Manager, Procurement Officer

Choose a creature within range who has previously agreed, in writing, to
hold your organization harmless. The triggering liability, damage, or blame
is redirected in full to the chosen creature, who must make a Solvency
saving throw. On a failed save, the creature suffers the full consequence
in your place. On a successful save, the creature suffers it anyway, but
may spend up to the next year attempting to recover a portion of it through
further litigation.

This spell has no effect on a creature who, at the time of signing, lacked
the legal sophistication or bargaining power to understand what they were
agreeing to. This limitation is rarely enforced and even less rarely
discovered before the contract is signed.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, the redirected liability may be spread across multiple
indemnifying parties simultaneously, reducing the consequence borne by any
one party but increasing the total time required to resolve it.
