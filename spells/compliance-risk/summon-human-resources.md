# Summon Human Resources

*2nd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though the summoned creature's actual arrival
is delayed 3 to 10 business days<br>
**Range/Area:** 30 feet, or the length of a scheduled meeting invite<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a formal complaint, filed through the proper
channel, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** 1 investigation, concluding whenever the summoned creature
determines it should<br>
**Classes:** Any Employee, Manager, Director

You summon one HR Representative to investigate a conflict, complaint, or
concern you specify at the time of casting. The representative is not
bound to your service and does not act on your behalf: it independently
determines a course of action based solely on minimizing the
organization's exposure to Solvency risk, which may or may not resemble
the outcome you were hoping for when you cast this spell.

A creature named in your complaint can be compelled to attend a meeting
with the summoned representative, but cannot be compelled to say anything
specific. You, the caster, are also a creature who can be so compelled,
and are equally unable to control what you say once summoned to that same
meeting.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, the summoned representative additionally creates a permanent,
Documented record of the investigation's existence (see Animate Legacy
System), though not necessarily of its findings.

---
**Flavor:** *"Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We take all
concerns seriously and will look into this."* — email, sent seven weeks
before the case is closed with no update provided to the original
complainant
