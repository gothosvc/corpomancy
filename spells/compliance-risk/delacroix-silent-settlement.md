# Delacroix's Silent Settlement

*5th-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, contingent on both parties' counsel finalizing
terms, typically weeks to months<br>
**Range/Area:** One creature or organization bringing a claim against
you<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a settlement payment, consumed; a Nondisclosure
spell, cast on the claimant as part of this casting)<br>
**Duration:** Permanent, so long as the underlying Nondisclosure holds<br>
**Classes:** General Counsel, CEO, Board

You resolve a claim, dispute, or allegation against your organization
through payment and binding, rather than through a finding on the
underlying merits. The claim is withdrawn, and any Discovery saving throw
later attempted against the underlying incident automatically fails,
unless the Nondisclosure component is independently broken.

This spell does not require the underlying incident to be false, resolved,
or even internally disputed by the caster — only that the claimant accept
payment in exchange for binding.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or
higher, you may cast this spell on a claim before it has been formally
filed, preventing it from ever entering any public record.

---
**Flavor:** *"The parties are pleased to have reached a mutually
satisfactory resolution and consider this matter closed."* — joint
statement, terms not disclosed
