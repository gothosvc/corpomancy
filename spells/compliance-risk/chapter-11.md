# Chapter 11

*9th-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range/Area:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a bankruptcy petition, consumed)
**Duration:** Until the reorganization plan is confirmed by the court,
commonly 12 to 24 months
**Classes:** General Counsel, CFO, board

Your organization becomes immune to all collection attempts, lawsuits, and
further Indemnification claims for the duration. All Amortized Loss and
outstanding severance damage owed is suspended, not forgiven — it resumes
accruing, with interest, the instant this spell ends. While it remains
active, you may compel any single creditor to accept a Reorganization Plan
of your choosing in place of what they are actually owed; a creditor may
attempt a Discovery saving throw to identify and object to a plan that pays
them meaningfully less than promised, but even a successful objection only
delays the plan — it does not restore the original terms.

This spell does not protect any individual executive from personal
liability incurred through fraud, and does not stop the Insolvent condition
from being publicly known.

***At Higher Levels.*** This spell's protection is total regardless of slot
level; a higher slot only shortens how long confirmation takes.

to employees, suppliers, and partners throughout this process."* — press
