# Golden Parachute

*3rd-level Compliance & Risk*

**Casting Time:** 1 action to negotiate, though it only functions if
written into the caster's employment contract before any dismissal or
change-of-control is triggered<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a severance clause, negotiated and signed;
typically consumes a portion of the caster's starting equity grant)<br>
**Duration:** Until triggered by the caster's own departure, voluntary or
otherwise, or until the underlying contract is renegotiated<br>
**Classes:** CEO, C-Suite, Board-Appointed Executive

You gain advantage on Tenure saving throws against any effect that would
remove you from your position, including Reduction in Force and Hostile
Takeover. If you are dismissed, resign under pressure, or are removed as
the result of a change of control, this spell triggers automatically: you
succeed on the associated saving throw regardless of the result rolled,
and you gain a Windfall equal to a number of years of your current
compensation, fixed at the time of signing rather than the time of
triggering.

The size of the Windfall is unaffected by the circumstances of your
departure, including your own performance, tenure, or responsibility for
the triggering event.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you may extend the advantage granted by this spell (but not the
automatic trigger or the Windfall) to a number of direct reports equal to
the slot level above 3rd.
