# Vendor Lock-In

*3rd-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, following a successful Three Bids and a Buy<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one vendor or supplier relationship<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a multi-year contract with an early-termination
penalty clause, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Until the contract term expires, commonly 3 to 5 years<br>
**Classes:** Procurement Officer, Category Manager

Touch one vendor relationship your organization currently holds. That
relationship becomes warded: for the duration, any rival organization
attempting to acquire the same vendor, or persuade your organization to
switch away from it, must succeed on a Discovery saving throw against the
early-termination penalty clause's true cost, deliberately obscured at
signing. On a failed save, the rival's attempt costs them additional
resources equal to the clause's penalty, paid to your vendor, not to you.

This spell does not prevent your organization from wanting to switch
vendors — only from being able to do so cheaply. Casters occasionally
forget this distinction until it is too late.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th
level or higher, the ward extends to prevent the vendor itself from
unilaterally renegotiating terms mid-contract, locking in pricing as well
as exclusivity.
