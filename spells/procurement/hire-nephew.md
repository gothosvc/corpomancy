# Hire Nephew

*2nd-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, no Headcount Approval check required<br>
**Range/Area:** Self<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a family relationship, and a job title invented
for the occasion)<br>
**Duration:** Until a creature with sufficient standing over the caster
notices and objects, which rarely occurs<br>
**Classes:** Founder, C-Suite, Board Member

You conjure one full-time employee, filling a budget line whether or not
one currently exists. Unlike Conjure Headcount, this spell is unaffected
by a Hiring Freeze and requires no Headcount Approval check of any kind.

The conjured employee's competence at the assigned role is determined
randomly rather than by any evaluated skill, and any resulting
disadvantage on relevant checks does not improve with onboarding, tenure,
or feedback.

While the caster retains sufficient standing within the organization, any
attempt by another creature to performance-manage, reassign, or dismiss
the conjured employee — including a Reduction in Force targeting their
team — automatically fails against that one creature, though it may
succeed normally against everyone else in the area of effect.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, you conjure one additional relative for each slot level above
2nd, each independently protected as described above.
