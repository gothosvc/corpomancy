# Power Word: Approved

*2nd-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Touch, one pending decision, requisition, purchase order,
or approval check, provided its cost or impact falls under the threshold
below<br>
**Components:** V (the requester's name, spoken by someone who already
knows the approver socially)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** VP, Founder, Anyone Who Golfs With the Approver

This spell requires no saving throw and bypasses any approval process it
targets entirely — Headcount Approval (see Conjure Headcount) included,
but not exclusively. Its effect is gated on two thresholds, both of which
must be met: whether the requester's relationship with the approver
crosses a familiarity line the approver would not describe out loud, and
whether the decision's cost or impact falls under some small fraction of
the organization's current Solvency — large enough to cover routine
requisitions, purchase orders, and headcount lines, but never openly
defined, and adjusted after the fact if anyone asks. Neither threshold
evaluates the decision's actual merit or business justification.

A decision whose cost exceeds this threshold cannot be approved by this
spell at any level, regardless of the caster's relationship to the
approver; it has to go through Bid War, Vertical Integration, or an
equivalent spell that actually engages with the organization's real
approval structure.

A request denied to another creature under ordinary process may still
succeed for you under this spell, cast on the identical request, without
either of you acknowledging that anything unusual occurred.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or
higher, the materiality threshold widens and the bypassed request may
retroactively apply to a Hiring Freeze already in effect, exempting it
after the fact — though it still cannot reach anything large enough to
require Board sign-off under any circumstances.
