# Bid War

*4th-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one contract, vendor relationship, or
acquisition target currently being pursued by a rival organization you can
identify as a competitor<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a bid, submitted above fair market value,
consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** CEO, CFO

Make an attack roll against the rival organization's Solvency, using your
bid amount as your attack bonus. On a hit, the rival loses access to the
contested resource entirely, regardless of its own bid, budget, or actual
need for it. You take damage equal to the difference between your winning
bid and the resource's real value to your organization, which is often
zero.

This spell does not require you to actually want the resource in question.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th
level or higher, you may target a contract currently up for renewal rather
than a new one, denying the rival a resource it already held.

---
**Flavor:** *"We're not sure why they wanted the lease on that building so
badly — they let it sit empty for two years."* — unnamed former executive
of the losing organization
