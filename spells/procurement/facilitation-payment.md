# Facilitation Payment

*5th-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, though it is never characterized internally as
payment for an official act<br>
**Range/Area:** One foreign government official or licensing body with
authority over a permit, contract, or customs clearance your organization
requires<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a sum of cash or its equivalent, consumed; an
invoice from a conjured Local Agent, describing services never
rendered)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous; the resulting permit, contract, or clearance
persists until formally revoked<br>
**Classes:** Regional Director, VP of International Operations, General
Counsel (retained to describe the arrangement as compliant)

You conjure one Contractor — a Local Agent — whose sole function is to
receive your payment and transmit an unspecified portion of it to the
target official. The Local Agent vanishes once the payment is transmitted,
along with any record of how much of it actually arrived. This spell
requires no saving throw; the target official's cooperation is granted
automatically, since the mechanism is structural rather than a test of
anyone's resistance.

Your organization must succeed on a Discovery saving throw whenever a
regulator in your home jurisdiction investigates the transaction. The save
is made with disadvantage if the Local Agent's invoice describes its
services in specific, verifiable detail, and with advantage if it does
not.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or
higher, you may route the payment through one additional Local Agent for
each slot level above 5th, reducing the disadvantage on the Discovery
saving throw by one step (minimum none) for each intermediary interposed.
