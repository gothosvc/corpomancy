# Three Bids and a Buy

*1st-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, plus a mandatory waiting period of 2 weeks
**Range/Area:** Special (any three vendors capable of being contacted)
**Components:** V, S, M (three request-for-proposal documents, only one of
which is sent in good faith)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Procurement Officer, Category Manager

You request formal proposals from three vendors of your choosing for a good
or service you have, in practice, already selected. At the conclusion of
the waiting period, you select the vendor of your choosing as the winner,
regardless of the content, price, or quality of the other two proposals.

This spell satisfies any organizational, legal, or auditory requirement for
competitive bidding, provided at least two vendors other than the intended
winner were contacted at all, even if neither was seriously considered. A
creature attempting to determine, after the fact, whether the process was
conducted in good faith must succeed on a Discovery saving throw; your
organization has advantage on this save if all three request-for-proposal
documents are retained on file (see Document Retention).

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or
higher, you may request proposals from additional vendors without changing
the outcome, further improving the appearance of rigor and granting a
further bonus to the Discovery saving throw's advantage.
