# Acquihire

*6th-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, contingent on a successful Headcount Approval
check for the entire acquired team at once<br>
**Range/Area:** Unlimited; one rival organization you can identify as a
competitor, and every employee of a single team within it<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (an acquisition agreement, consumed; a retention
bonus per acquired employee, not consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous<br>
**Classes:** CEO, CFO, Board

Choose one team within a rival organization. Each employee of that team
must succeed on a Tenure saving throw or Convert into your organization's
employees at the start of the next pay period — exactly as Poach, but
resolved simultaneously for the whole team rather than as individual
castings. You are not required to retain, use, or even name the product
the team was building; only the people.

A Bus Factor already in effect on any departing creature triggers normally
against the rival organization, in addition to any damage dealt by this
spell directly.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th
level or higher, you may additionally acquire the underlying intellectual
property the team was working on, though this component costs a separate,
considerably larger sum not reflected above.

**Flavor:** *"We're thrilled to welcome this incredibly talented team to
our organization. We won't be continuing their previous product, but we're
excited about what they'll build here."* — acquisition announcement, six
months before the product is shut down and half the team has already left
