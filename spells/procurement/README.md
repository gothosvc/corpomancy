# Procurement & Resourcing

*Classical tradition: Conjuration*

Field notes on Conjuration as practiced here: summoning something from
nothing — headcount, budget, contractors, vendors, office supplies.
Roughly half of what we've catalogued is about conjuring the resource
itself; the other half is about the ritual cost of getting it approved,
which several of us privately suspect is the real spell.

**Field tone:** transactional, faintly desperate, full of forms. The
caster is always negotiating with a bureaucracy as much as with reality.

**Vocabulary recorded in the field:** requisition, headcount approval,
RFP, vendor onboarding, budget line, backfill, statement of work, purchase
order, contractor conversion, three bids and a buy, capex vs. opex, lead
time, sole source justification, acquihire, talent lift-out, nepotism
hire, winner's curse, related-party transaction.
