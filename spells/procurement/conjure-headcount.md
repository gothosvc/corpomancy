# Conjure Headcount

*3rd-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, contingent on a successful Headcount Approval
check
**Range/Area:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (an approved requisition form, consumed; a budget
line, occupied but not consumed, for the duration)
**Duration:** Until the position is eliminated by a Reduction in Force or
ended by attrition
**Classes:** Hiring Manager, VP of Talent, Finance Business Partner

You conjure one full-time employee, filling an open budget line. The
Headcount Approval check is made with advantage if backed by attrition, a
clear backfill justification, or an executive champion, and with
disadvantage otherwise. Unlike a Contractor, a conjured employee gains full
access to your organization's systems, benefits, and institutional
knowledge over a 90-day Onboarding period, during which it operates at
disadvantage.

The 1-action casting time listed above is rarely accurate in practice: the
Headcount Approval check typically takes between 4 and 12 additional weeks
to resolve, during which your team continues operating without the
conjured employee.

This spell automatically fails, with no component refunded, if a Hiring
Freeze is in effect at the time of casting.

***At Higher Levels.*** When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or
higher, you conjure one additional employee for each slot level above 3rd,
each independently requiring its own Headcount Approval check and
Onboarding period.
