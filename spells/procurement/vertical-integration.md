# Vertical Integration

*7th-level Procurement & Resourcing*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, contingent on successful Headcount Approval
and Discovery checks for every acquired link (a Supply Chain Audit)<br>
**Range/Area:** Special; every vendor or supplier relationship currently
under a Conjure Contractor or Three Bids and a Buy arrangement with your
organization<br>
**Components:** V, S, M (a capital expenditure budget, consumed; an
acquisition agreement per target, consumed)<br>
**Duration:** Permanent<br>
**Classes:** COO, VP of Operations, Chief Procurement Officer

You conjure ownership of one or more organizations currently supplying
goods or services to you, converting each from an external vendor into a
wholly owned subsidiary. Each acquired organization's Contractors
automatically Convert into full-time employees of your organization at no
further cost, and any Lead Time previously imposed by that link in the
chain is eliminated.

This spell does not eliminate the underlying costs of production, staffing,
and maintenance the vendor was previously absorbing; it only moves those
costs onto your own balance sheet, where they show up as a permanent
increase to your organization's Amortized Loss rather than as a line-item
expense.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th
level or higher, you may target one additional link in the supply chain
for each slot level above 7th.

costs and improve quality control."* — investor call transcript, eighteen
